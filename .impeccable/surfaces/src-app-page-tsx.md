## Direction contract

THESIS: The name is the product. A monumental dark typographic nameplate where
"KUSHAL KRISHNAPPA" fills the first viewport as a living signature (it doubles as
an embeddable iframe signature for recruiter outreach). It refuses the default
dev-portfolio template — a centered hero headline over a row of equal project
cards — and refuses a light theme entirely.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground (#0A0A0A), bone text (#ECECEA), a single restrained
cool accent (~#4DD4E0, Go-adjacent) used only for the live "now" dot, chip
borders, links, and the primary action. A heavy grotesk display face with real
presence sets the giant wordmark (NOT Inter/Helvetica/Arial defaults — pick a
committed display grotesk); monospace for labels, meta, and chips; pill keyword
chips; hairline (#1E1E1E) dividers; an optional custom cursor. Recognizable with
all content removed by the sheer scale of the wordmark on black.

STORY: A recruiter or engineer lands; the name resolves with a split-text reveal
(the signature); they immediately read "backend & platform engineer — invisible
infrastructure for visible impact," see current status, stack chips, and proof
metrics, and can contact or download the résumé. Scrolling reveals the systems
(alert-bell, auto-sim-rehab, AI-identity ownership), experience, projects,
writing, and the research role.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A giant two-line wordmark (KUSHAL / KRISHNAPPA), left-anchored,
filling the frame, revealed by a staggered split-text animation that settles to a
plainly legible name (must still dominate at small/iframe sizes). A thin role line
sits beneath. Top bar: a "KK" mark left, nav links right, a cyan "contact" action
top-right (the primary action). Bottom-left: a live "now" line + keyword chips;
bottom-right: proof metrics (5h→10m MTTR, 9,000+ VMs, mutation-tested).

FORM: Monumental Nameplate (editorial masthead / display wordmark). My #1 grounded
candidate, chosen by the user as IMPECCABLE'S PICK over the roll's assigned
Machined Steel Nameplate. seed:300497bb2ab272a7

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish
review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
